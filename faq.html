Merchant FAQS

What is Onward?

Onward is your digital shopping concierge. By adding Onward to your order, you unlock VIP benefits that ensure an amazing post-purchase experience.

With Onward, you'll be 100% covered against lost, stolen, and damaged packages. We'll also make your delivery carbon neutral and resolve any quality issues with your products.

Why do I need Onward?

Onward improves customer satisfaction while reducing support requests and refund costs.

Does Onward cover orders that are shipped internationally?

Yes, eligible international shipments can also be protected depending on the merchant plan.

How do I report an order issue?

Customers can report issues directly from their order confirmation page.

How long will it take to resolve my claim?

Most claims are resolved within a few business days.

What is Onward CashBack?

Cashback rewards customers for eligible purchases.

What is the Onward Satisfaction Guarantee?

The Satisfaction Guarantee provides additional confidence for eligible orders.

What is the Onward Returns App?

The Returns App simplifies product returns and exchanges for customers.

What is the Order Editing App?

The Order Editing App lets customers update orders before shipment.
